Our Story

Built on Compassion, Backed by Medicine

STACKLY Veterinary Clinic began in 2011 with a simple belief: pets deserve the same standard of care, dignity, and attention we'd want for any family member. What started as a single consultation room has grown into a full-service hospital spanning diagnostics, surgery, dentistry, and 24/7 emergency care.

Today, our team of 40+ specialists serves over 12,000 pet families, guided by the same founding philosophy — modern medicine, delivered gently.

Our Journey

From One Room to a Full-Service Hospital

Fifteen years of growth, guided by the same standard of care we opened our doors with.

2011

STACKLY opens as a single-room consultation clinic with two veterinarians.

2015

Surgical suite and in-house diagnostics lab open, ending referral wait times.

2019

24/7 emergency department launches alongside our pet boarding wing.

2024

STACKLY crosses 12,000 pet families served with 40+ specialists on staff.
